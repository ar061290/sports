Recovery principles

The lessons that cost the most to learn.

Time under tension beats rep counts

Slow, controlled negatives built more strength than rushing through numbers to say I did them.

Restraint is a skill

Growth is limited less by effort than by patience. Counting days rested made rest feel like training.

Adapt, don't skip

If the perfect cool-down isn't possible, do the parts that are. A partial routine beats none.

One outlet isn't enough

Training is a great way to manage stress, but it works best as one support among many: friends, rest, creativity and conversation.
