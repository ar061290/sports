    Recovery principles

    Lessons that usually cost an injury to learn. They are here so you can learn them for free.

    Time under tension beats rep counts

    Slow, controlled reps and negatives build more strength than rushing through numbers just to say you did them.

    Restraint is a skill

    Progress is limited less by effort than by patience. Counting the days you rest makes rest feel like training, because it is.

    Adapt, don't skip

    If the perfect warm-up or cool-down isn't possible, do the parts that are. A partial routine beats none.

    One outlet isn't enough

    Training is a great way to manage stress, but it works best as one support among many: friends, sleep, creativity and conversation.

    A template, not a rule. Type straight into each day, and tick rest days. It should bend around exams, injuries and everything else in your life.
